## Reading this as a work order

The percentage is not a score. Of the unapplied settings, the ones that change a single-extruder print are the per-feature speeds, the cooling curves, the Z-hop shaping, the support details and the seam shapes. The rest are multi-material, identity metadata, emission options or a stock preset's menu defaults: a printer with one extruder cannot honour the first group however long the engine is worked on, and honouring the second changes no output.
